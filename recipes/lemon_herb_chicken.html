Simple Lemon Herb Chicken



Description

This simple lemon herb chicken is a quick and delicious meal made with tender chicken breasts, fresh lemon juice, and aromatic herbs.
It's easy to prepare and perfect for a light lunch or dinner.


Ingredients

  • 2 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
  • 1 medium lemon
  • Salt to taste
  • Freshly ground black pepper to taste
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 pinch dried oregano
  • 2 sprigs fresh parsley, for garnish

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Cut the lemon in half.
  3. Squeeze the juice from half of the lemon over the chicken breasts.
  4. Season the chicken with salt and let it rest while preparing the skillet.
  5. Heat the olive oil in a small skillet over medium-low heat.
  6. When the oil is hot, place the chicken breasts into the skillet.
  7. While cooking the chicken, add the juice from the remaining half of the lemon.
  8. Season with black pepper and dried oregano.
  9. Sauté the chicken for approximately 5 to 10 minutes on each side, or until fully cooked and the juices run clear.
  10. Remove the chicken from the skillet and garnish with fresh parsley.
  11. Serve immediately and enjoy!
